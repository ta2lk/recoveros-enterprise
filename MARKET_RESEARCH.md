# RecoverOS - Enterprise Revenue Recovery Market Research

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Validation

Accounts Payable (AP) departments and corporate treasury divisions handle millions of financial transactions annually. Due to ERP migrations, fragmented procurement workflows, disparate billing formats, and manual data entry errors, substantial capital is lost to verified financial leakage.

Rather than being a speculative problem, revenue recovery and accounts payable audit is a mature, high-demand enterprise sector where corporations actively budget and contract third-party audit firms and SaaS platforms.

---

## 2. Key Industry Statistics & Ground-Truth Benchmarks

### Accounts Payable Overpayments & Duplicate Disbursements
*   **Leakage Proportion**: The **American Productivity & Quality Center (APQC)** and the **Institute of Finance and Management (IOFM)** report that organizations lose between **0.8% and 2.0%** of total accounts payable disbursements to duplicate or erroneous payments ([APQC Benchmark Study](https://www.apexanalytix.com); [IOFM AP Metrics Report](https://www.expensepoint.com)).
*   **Duplicate Frequency**: Duplicate payments occur on **0.1% to 0.8%** of all processed commercial invoices across mid-market and enterprise firms ([Peakflo](https://peakflo.co); [Transparent Global](https://transparentglobal.com)).
*   **Missed Prompt-Payment Discounts**: Up to **82%** of early settlement terms (e.g. 2/10 Net 30) are forfeited due to invoice approval latency or ledger mismatch, despite payments frequently being disbursed within the 10-day window.

### Logistics & Freight Billing Inaccuracies
*   **Invoice Discrepancies**: According to **Cass Information Systems** (which processes and pays over 51 million invoices annually across 185 countries) and **Loop Technologies**, between **1% and 5%** of total corporate freight spend contains billing inaccuracies ([Cass Information Systems](https://www.cassinfo.com); [Loop DUX Whitepaper](https://www.loop.com)).
*   **Carrier Error Rate**: Industry logistics audits report that up to **20%** of freight bills contain errors, predominantly consisting of unapproved accessorial fees, duplicate bill-of-lading entries, or inaccurate fuel surcharges ([STB Freight](https://stbfreight.com); [Senvo AI](https://senvo.ai)).
*   **Net Recoverable Spend**: Freight Audit and Payment (FAP) services routinely recover between **2% and 8%** of audited transportation spend directly back to corporate accounts.

## 3. Commercial Contingency Models & Market Pricing

The dominant business model in recovery audit is the **Contingency Success Fee**:
*   **Commercial AP Recovery Audits**: Providers historically charge a contingency fee ranging between **20% and 30%** of successfully settled funds ([Apex Analytix](https://www.apexanalytix.com)). There is zero upfront cost to the enterprise client, making procurement approval frictionless.
*   **Government & Healthcare RAC Audits**: Medicare Recovery Audit Contractors (RACs) operate at **10.86% to 12.50%** contingency fees established by statutory bidding ([Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov); [Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services](https://healthlawalliance.com)).
*   **RecoverOS Value Proposition**: By leveraging autonomous multi-agent reconciliation with deterministic code math, RecoverOS reduces operating overhead, enabling a standard **20% contingency fee** with high-volume enterprise discounting down to **12% – 18%**.
